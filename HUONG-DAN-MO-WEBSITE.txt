CACH MO WEBSITE GEARTREKCO
=========================

1. Nham dup vao file "Mo website.cmd".
2. Cho vai giay, website se tu dong mo trong trinh duyet.
3. Giu cua so mau den dang chay trong khi xem website.
4. Khi xem xong, dong cua so mau den de tat may chu xem thu.

Luu y:
Khong nham dup truc tiep vao index.html trong thu muc dist.
Website React can duoc mo qua may chu cuc bo de JavaScript va cac duong dan hoat dong dung.
